# Privacy Policy — Tempo

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026
**Last updated:** 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tempo app ("Tempo", "the app", "we", "us")
handles your information. It is provided by Gerhard De Nazare
("the developer").

> **Short version:** Tempo stores everything you create — your workouts,
> sessions, workout history, custom exercises, and settings — **on your own
> device**. We have no accounts and no server that collects your content, and we
> do not sell or share your personal information. Your data only leaves your
> device when **you** choose to share or export it. The one exception is the
> optional Tempo Pro purchase, which is handled by Google Play and our billing
> provider (RevenueCat) — see Section 6.

---

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to the Tempo mobile app distributed through the Google Play
Store (and, where applicable, other platforms).

## 2. Data we collect

**We do not collect personal data on our servers. Tempo has no user accounts
and no backend that receives your data.**

The content you create and use in the app is stored **locally on your device**
using the device's standard app storage. This includes:

- Workouts you build or save (Intervals, Circuit, AMRAP, For Time, EMOM)
- Sessions (chains of workouts)
- Workout history (your completed-workout log; capped at the most recent
  entries on your device)
- Custom exercises you create, including any image or animation you add from
  your device's gallery
- App settings and preferences (sound, voice cues, vibration, tags, and
  similar options)

This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us. If you
delete the app, this on-device data is removed by the operating system in the
normal way.

## 3. Device permissions

Tempo requests the following device permissions only when a feature needs them,
and only for the purpose described:

- **Photos / media (gallery):** Requested **only** when you choose to add your
  own image or animation to a custom exercise. The selected image is stored
  locally with that exercise. Tempo does not browse, upload, or scan your photo
  library beyond the single item you pick.
- **Notifications:** Requested so the app can alert you when a timer or workout
  phase completes. These are **local notifications** scheduled on your device.
  Tempo does not use push notifications and does not send your device a message
  from any server.

Tempo does **not** request access to your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, or precise identifiers.

## 4. Information you choose to share or export

Tempo includes features that act **only when you initiate them**:

- **Share / Export:** When you tap Share or Export, Tempo creates a file (for
  example, a workout, a session, or a full backup) and hands it to your
  device's standard share sheet. **You** decide where it goes (for example, a
  messaging app, email, or file storage). Tempo does not choose a destination
  and does not upload anything automatically. For exercises from Tempo's
  built-in catalog, exported files contain only a reference to the exercise,
  not its bundled image/animation.
- **Import:** When you import a file you previously saved or received, Tempo
  reads the file you select and stores its contents locally on your device.
- **Opening links:** Some items (such as a link-based session, or a reference
  URL on an exercise) can contain a web address you entered. When you tap it,
  your device opens that address in your normal web browser. Any data handling
  from that point is governed by the browser and the destination website, not
  by Tempo.

Because these actions are under your control, you are responsible for where you
send data when you use them.

## 5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Tempo does **not** include advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking technologies.
We do not build advertising profiles and do not track you across apps or
websites. The only third-party service Tempo integrates is **RevenueCat**, used
solely to process the optional in-app purchase described in Section 6 — not for
analytics or advertising.

## 6. Purchases (Tempo Pro)

Tempo offers a single one-time "Tempo Pro" unlock. When you choose to buy it:

- **The purchase is processed by Google Play**, not by Tempo. Your payment
  details (card, billing information) are handled entirely by Google — Tempo
  never sees or stores them.
- **We use RevenueCat** as our in-app purchase provider. RevenueCat works with
  Google Play to confirm the purchase, remember that you own Pro, and let you
  restore it on a new device. To do this, RevenueCat processes purchase-related
  information such as a purchase token/receipt and an app-generated anonymous
  identifier. It does not receive your payment card details.
- **What Tempo itself receives** is only an entitlement signal — i.e. whether
  "Pro" is active for the current purchase — which is used to unlock the paid
  features. We do not receive your name, email, or payment information from the
  purchase.
- **Restoring purchases:** because the unlock is tied to your Google account via
  Google Play, reinstalling the app or using a new device restores Pro
  automatically (and a "Restore purchases" option is available in Settings).

The processing done by these providers is governed by their own privacy
policies:

- Google Play / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Tempo does not use purchase information for advertising or tracking.

## 7. Children

Tempo is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed at children. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children,
because the app does not collect personal data on our servers.

## 8. Data security and retention

Your data is stored on your device and protected by your device's own security
(such as your screen lock and the operating system's app sandboxing). Because we
do not hold your data on a server, retention is entirely under your control:
delete individual items in the app, clear your history, or uninstall the app to
remove its local data.

## 9. Your choices

- Delete any workout, session, custom exercise, or history entry from within
  the app.
- Clear all workout history from the app's settings.
- Uninstall the app to remove its locally stored data from your device.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will update
the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at the same public URL.
